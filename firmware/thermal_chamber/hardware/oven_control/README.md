# Oven control circuit (KiCad 7)

The breadboard that drives the oven: Arduino Nano 33 IoT, NTC, TIP31 and the LOTI 48D25A solid-state
relay. Open `forno_controle.kicad_pro` in KiCad; the PDF, SVG and PNG are the same sheet.

Drawn by the colleague on 2026-10-29 from the firmware, the TCC and photos of the breadboard, not
from measurements; what the photos did not show is in dashed boxes. `gerador/` is the script that
generated the sheet. Details, in Portuguese, in `LEIAME.md`. Moved here from branch `dt2027` on
2026-11-16. Safety backstops for the oven are in `../../SAFETY.md`.
